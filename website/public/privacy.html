Privacy

Last updated September 2026.

This is a static research website published by StarDrive Inc. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels, and no forms.

The only external requests the pages make are to Google Fonts for typefaces and to the jsDelivr CDN for the KaTeX math renderer and, on two visualization pages, the D3 library. Those providers' own privacy policies apply to those requests. Links to GitHub and to thestardrive.com leave this site.

PDF downloads are direct file transfers from this server and are not logged by us beyond the hosting provider's standard access logs.
