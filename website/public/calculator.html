What to notice

  • 1/α depends on p only through ln p, so it is insensitive: a factor of 2 in p moves it by about 16. That is why four unrelated relations can all "point to" a prime near 10⁵ without any of them being precise.
  • The angles that depend only on z (the CP phase) do not move when you change p at all. The framework's most striking agreements are its least adjustable ones.
  • The mass ratios are pure combinatorics of z. They are the easiest thing on this page to get suspicious about, and the project now agrees.
