## 3. Global partition and exact positive remainder

For all subsets J of the binomial(N,2) pair edges define the sine/cosine
product chi_J using theta=pi/2 below R/8 and0 above R/4. The global product
identity is sum_J chi_J²=1. On Omega_N, active edges form a matching because
two incident short edges force a forbidden triple. Hence only matching components

cosine factor is0, so the all-pair potential on chi_(P,a)psi equals precisely
the selected matching potential. Full kinetic energy is retained.

For an unordered COMPLETE configuration edge{n,m}, define

    D_a(n,m)=sum_J|chi_J(an)-chi_J(am)|²,

## 4. Fixed-N global multiplier bound and convergence

The partition vector is a tensor product of unit vectors (sin theta_e,cos theta_e).
Each derivative term is orthogonal to terms differentiating another factor,
because a unit vector is orthogonal to its derivative. Therefore

    sum_J|partial_l chi_J|²=sum_e|partial_l theta_e|².

For N>=1 each scalar particle coordinate affects at most N-1 angles, so this
is <=(N-1)L². Integral Cauchy-Schwarz along an edge gives

    0<=w_a<=3kappa N(N-1)L²=M_N.                        (6)

derivatives make W_glob globally Lipschitz. Cell radius sqrt(3N)a/2 then gives

    ||tilde(w_a)-W_glob||_infinity->0, 0<=W_glob<=M_N.   (7)

All such constants may depend on N. On the open allowed region nonmatching

## 6. All triple constraints and global domain identification

Inside a centered physical cell every particle moves from its node by at most
sqrt(3)a/2. Every pair distance and every triple diameter change by at most
sqrt(3)a. For any one of the binomial(N,3) triples T, an allowed-node cell
