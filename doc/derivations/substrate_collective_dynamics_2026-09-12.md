## Classical expansion to verify independently

The supplied energy is Hcl=-C*sum_x(psi_x.conjugate()*psi_(x+1)+h.c.)+(g/2)*sum_x |psi_x|^4. At uniform density the constrained stationary frequency is mu=-2C+g*nbar. In the fixed-norm tangent space, expansion gives

H2=(1/2)*sum_k[(g+epsilon_k/(2*nbar))*|delta n_k|²+2*nbar*epsilon_k*|theta_k|²], epsilon_k=4*C*sin²(k/2).

The two variables are a conjugate pair, not two independent displacement fields. Independent differentiation of the existing DNLS equation with psi=sqrt(n)*exp(+i theta) gives dot n=+dH/dtheta and dot theta=-dH/dn, using the rotating energy H-mu*N. Put alpha_k=g+epsilon_k/(2*nbar) and beta_k=2*nbar*epsilon_k. Then dot delta n=beta_k*theta and dot theta=-alpha_k*delta n. The squared mode frequency is alpha_k*beta_k=epsilon_k*(epsilon_k+2*g*nbar). For an external perturbation +sum f_x*n_x, chi_nn(z)=beta_k/(z²-alpha_k*beta_k); its static sign is negative. The positive static susceptibility to -sum f_x*n_x is1/alpha_k on nonzero modes. beta_k is the phase Hessian coefficient, not a physical elastic modulus. At k0 the density fluctuation is removed by the fixed norm and uniform phase is a symmetry coordinate. The formal unconstrained k0 density cost is not a dynamical fixed-norm oscillator. A strong-g classical expansion is not assumed to approximate the strong-g quantum ground state.

## Quantum rank-one projection to verify independently

Let |G> be a resolved nondegenerate ground of the unchanged complete quantum H. Put A=H-E0, v=(rho_k-<rho_k>)|G>, s=v†v and e=v/sqrt(s) if s>0. Set P=e*e† and Q=I-P. In an orthonormal basis W for ranQ define a=e†Ae, b=W†Ae and B=W†AW. The exact source resolvent is

F(z)=v†(z-A)^(-1)v=s/[z-a-Sigma(z)], Sigma(z)=b†(z-B)^(-1)b.

The inverse is restricted to ranQ: do not invert a full Q A Q expression and accidentally add the removed direction. The finite memory kernel is K(t)=b†exp(-i*B*t)b. It is a discrete finite-system quantity. It does not by itself derive irreversible relaxation, a Markov approximation or the continuum phason coefficient Gamma. A thermodynamic/coarse-graining argument and a microscopic internal-coordinate construction would be additional work.

F is a positive-excitation resolvent, not the full retarded commutator response. For source O, define F_O using (O-<O>)|G>. Then module2's convention is chi_(O,O†)(z)=F_(O†)(z)+F_O(-z)=F_(O†)(z)+conjugate(F_O(-conjugate(z))). The last form evaluates both resolvents in the upper half-plane. Both adjoint-source channels must be computed unless equality is separately justified; parity of the frozen zero-flux ring is not a generic non-Hermitian-source identity. Verify against independent full-space resolvents and spectra rather than only the projected expression. Zero source and b=0 require explicit controls, not arbitrary small-value clipping. Eigenpair, basis orthogonality and inverse-conditioning screens are heuristic numerical diagnostics, not exact error certificates.

## Independently derived bounds and controls

Write beta=||b|| and eta=Im z>0. Hermiticity gives Im Sigma=-eta*||(z-B)^(-1)b||²<=0, |Sigma|<=beta²/eta, Im(z-a-Sigma)>=eta and |F|<=s/eta. There are no exact upper-half-plane poles. Numerical inverse failures there indicate arithmetic or conditioning, not physical poles. The variance identity beta²=<e|A²|e>-a² is an independent check; compute beta directly from the residual rather than subtracting nearly equal moments.

With c(t)=<e|exp(-iAt)|e>, elimination gives dot c=-i*a*c-integral_0^t K(t-u)c(u)du, c(0)=1. Transform signs are Sigma=-i*integral_0^infinity exp(izt)K(t)dt and F=-i*s*integral_0^infinity exp(izt)c(t)dt. Exact bounds include |K(t)|<=beta², K(0)=beta², K(-t)=conjugate(K(t)); |c(t)-exp(-iat)|<=min(2,beta²*t²/2), complementary leakage<=min(1,beta²*t²), and |F-s/(z-a)|<=s*beta²/[eta*|z-a|²]<=s*beta²/eta³, using the centered spectral second moment. These sufficient bounds may be loose; failing a desired bound does not prove the approximation inaccurate.

At fixed real frequency, changing eta to eta' gives |F_eta-F_eta'|<=s*|eta-eta'|/(eta*eta') and |Sigma_eta-Sigma_eta'|<=beta²*|eta-eta'|/(eta*eta'). Truncating either transform at T gives tail bounds s*exp(-eta*T)/eta and beta²*exp(-eta*T)/eta respectively. A floating solve residual r=b-(z-B)xhat gives a diagnostic version of |Sigmahat-Sigma|<=beta*||r||/eta; a certificate would require certified inputs/residual norms, which are not supplied here.

Grouping exact B eigenvalues, K(t)=sum_lambda kappa_lambda*exp(-i lambda t), kappa_lambda=||Pi_lambda b||². Its infinite-time mean squared magnitude is sum kappa_lambda²>0 for b!=0. Thus nonzero finite closed-system memory cannot tend permanently to zero. This does not exclude approximate damping over a finite window, thermodynamic limits or justified coarse-graining. Numerical degeneracy grouping is descriptive, not an exact infinite-time certificate.

Independent toy oracle A=diag(0,1,3), e=(0,1,1)/sqrt(2) gives a=2,beta²=1,Sigma=1/(z-2),F=(z-2)/[(z-1)(z-3)],K=exp(-2it). Its self-energy pole at2 is a source-resolvent zero, not an excitation pole. The distinct one-transition source gives b=0 and an undamped single line. A zero source has F=0 but normalized projection and Sigma are not applicable.

For the classical independent oracle put psi=exp(-i*mu*t)*(sqrt(nbar)+u+i*v). Then dot u=C*Laplacian*v and dot v=-(C*Laplacian+2*g*nbar*I)*u, with delta n=2*sqrt(nbar)*u and theta=v/sqrt(nbar). Real-space Hessians are Kn=gI+C*Laplacian/(2*nbar), Ktheta=2*C*nbar*Laplacian. After fixed norm and global-phase quotient there are L-1 real oscillators. At g0 all nonzero modes remain regular with omega=epsilon and positive static susceptibility2*nbar/epsilon. Gradient stiffness2*C*nbar and total-flux curvature2*C*nbar/L are different normalizations; neither identifies the interacting quantum curvature.

## Frozen API and numerical contract

The generic projected_system(H,source) takes a vector, not an operator. It may be nonconnected; in that case it computes the full A=H-E0 resolvent including ground weight and is explicitly not an excitation-only observable. The quantum ring wrapper supplies genuinely connected density and adjoint-source vectors on one reused original eigensystem. Internal fields include system,A,source,s,e,W,a,b,B,beta_squared and complementary eigendata/diagnostics. Zero source has a separate status: F=0; normalized Sigma and K are not applicable and their evaluators raise. Validate frequency even before structural-zero returns. Nonzero one-transition sources have b=0 with exact zero memory, not decay. Times may be signed finite real for K(-t) checks; frequencies must have finite normal components and positive imaginary part.

Classical modes report epsilon,alpha,beta,omega_squared,omega and positive static susceptibility; k0 fixed-norm response is structurally zero and compressibility is not computed by division. Inputs L3..5,nbar>0,C>0,g>=0. Generic matrix/vector shape and cap precede conversion/allocation; unsupported overflow/underflow or unresolved arithmetic must not manufacture zeros. Exact fallback evaluates the supplied floating data, not exact eigenpairs. Demos contain classical_cases, quantum_cases, toy_controls and limitations with strict finite JSON.
