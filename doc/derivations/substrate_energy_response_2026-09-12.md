## Local transfer derivation

Write U_x=g*n_x(n_x-1)/2 and h_x=U_x+aV_(x-1)+bV_x with(a,b)=(1/2,1/2) or(1/4,3/4). The complete hopping bilinears satisfy [a†_i a_j,a†_l a_m]=delta_(j,l)*a†_i a_m-delta_(m,i)*a†_l a_j. Define Q_x=i[V_x,V_(x+1)]=i*C²*(a†_x a_(x+2)-a†_(x+2) a_x) and

A_x=a*i[U_x,V_x]+b*i[V_x,U_(x+1)]+a²*Q_(x-1)+b²*Q_x.

Expanding all local overlaps gives J_(x->y)=A_x*delta_(y,x+1)-A_y*delta_(x,y+1)+a*b*(Q_x*delta_(y,x+2)-Q_y*delta_(x,y+2)), with all Kronecker conditions moduloL and all coincident terms added. The two squared coefficients in A_x are not a*b: neighboring local terms share differently weighted consecutive bonds. For the symmetric partition the onsite part simplifies to (g/2)*(n_x+n_(x+1)-1)*j_x, commuting with that total bond occupation. For L3 the symmetric adjacent current is A_x-Q_(x+1)/4; for L4 the opposite-site current is(Q_x-Q_(x+2))/4=Q_x/2. Thus deleting distance2 terms or overwriting colliding modular contributions changes the continuity equation.

Summing the antisymmetric pair current gives dot h_x=A_(x-1)-A_x+a*b*(Q_(x-2)-Q_x). Fourier-transforming this difference rather than dividing by momentum gives R_k=dot h_k=q_k*A_k+a*b*q_(2k)*Q_k, q_k=exp(-ik)-1. At k0 this is structurally zero; no division by q0 is used. These A and Q components are a local continuity decomposition, not independent fields or physical propagation modes.

For delta h_x=B_x-B_(x-1), B_x=V_x/4, the primed quantities satisfy h'_k=h_k+d_k*V_k/4 and R'_k=R_k+d_k*dot V_k/4, d_k=1-exp(-ik). Pair currents transform as J'_(x->y)=J_(x->y)+i[h_x,delta h_y]+i[delta h_x,h_y]+i[delta h_x,delta h_y]. Their sums preserve total energy but the finite-k local operators do not agree. Independent adversarial verification of these formulas and the response identities is required before numerical acceptance.

## Response, spectral moments and partition comparison

For general operators E,F the unchanged retarded convention is chi_EF(z)=sum_(n>0)[E_0n*F_n0/(z-Delta_n)-F_0n*E_n0/(z+Delta_n)], Imz>0, with Delta_n=E_n-E_0. Both numerators are retained. Integration by parts, equivalently the actual-gap spectral identities, gives z*chi_EF=<[E,F]>+i*chi_(dot E,F)=<[E,F]>-i*chi_(E,dot F). Local energy uses E=h_k and dot E=R_k, never a phenomenological nearest-neighbor current replacing R_k. The equal-time contact is part of the Ward identity, not an optional subtraction.

For E=h_k define Mplus=sum Delta_n*|E_n0|² and Mminus=sum Delta_n*|E_0n|². The double commutator <[E,[H,E†]]>=Mplus+Mminus; identifying its half with either unsymmetrized moment requires equality of the two measures. Do not assume that equality for arbitrary non-Hermitian sources. The real zero-flux ring admits a real unique ground and the energy partitions used here have real local matrices, which supplies the needed paired Fourier symmetry. With R=dot E the contact <[R,E†]>=-i*(Mplus+Mminus), and the second Ward identity reads z²*chi_(E,E†)=z*<[E,E†]>+i*<[R,E†]>+chi_(R,R†). The first contact vanishes for these real local energy sources and their real ground, but it is still checked independently.

For the symmetric partition, site reflection sends h_k to h_-k whereas delta h_k=d_k*V_k/4 goes to -delta h_-k. Let P_Delta be a complete exact energy projector and c_Delta=<G|h_k† P_Delta delta h_k|G>. Reflection together with occupation-basis conjugation and a real invariant ground gives c_Delta=-conj(c_Delta). Thus the cross term is purely imaginary and drops from the summed squared source norm. Reflection alone is insufficient, and individual mixed susceptibilities need not vanish. The independently reviewed additional check is therefore chi_(h'_k,h'_-k)=chi_(h_k,h_-k)+|d_k|²*chi_(V_k,V_-k)/16, rather than partition invariance. The same statement uses energy-projector weights, not arbitrary labels inside degenerate eigenspaces. For frozen L3,m1 the coefficient is3/16. Positivity of Perron-Frobenius ground occupation amplitudes and the nonzero factor cos(pi/3) in V_k|G> imply a strict negative difference at z=.5i for both frozen g values. This predetermined witness was independently reviewed before evaluation; it was not selected by inspecting results.

H[f]=sum f_x*h_x is an externally specified weighted-energy perturbation. The continuity and Ward identities describe that chosen microscopic observable.
