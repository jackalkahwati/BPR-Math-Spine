## Sources, signed thresholds and spectral measures

Use a_k=L^(-1/2) sum_x exp(-ikx)a_x, k=2*pi*m/L. For adjacent complete sectors, annihilation has matrix entry sqrt(n_x) from n to n-e_x. Addition on H_N is the adjoint of annihilation from H_(N+1), with the conjugate Fourier phase. Its adjoint is NOT an annihilation matrix with un-conjugated coefficients reused as creation. All basis ordering is read from actual occupation tuples.

Let |G_N> be the resolved fixed-N ground state. Define removal source v_-=a_k|G_N>, addition source v_+=a_k†|G_N>. For the full eigensystems in N±1, W_± are squared overlaps and d_±=E_alpha(N±1)-E0(N). These d_± may be signed. Report mu_plus=E0(N+1)-E0(N), mu_minus=E0(N)-E0(N-1), Delta_c=mu_plus-mu_minus. The removal ground-state energy cost is -mu_minus, not mu_minus. A threshold can be dark at a particular momentum; do not identify a sector ground-energy difference with a guaranteed visible spectral line.

With the ring translation taking occupation tuples (n0,...,n_(L-1)) to (n_(L-1),n0,...), the rectangular maps satisfy T_(N-1)*A_k^(N)=exp(ik)*A_k^(N)*T_N. The adjoint addition transforms with exp(-ik). This independently checks the momentum labeling, which spectral reflection alone would not detect.

Integrated removal weight is <a_k†a_k>=n_k, addition weight is 1+n_k. Thus Wplus_total-Wminus_total=1. First moments are v_±†(H_(N±1)-E0(N))v_±, using actual energies rather than descriptive grouped representatives. For Im z>0 the retarded commutator Green function is G_k(z)=sum Wplus/(z-d_plus)-sum Wminus/(z+d_minus). This follows -i theta(t)<[a_k(t),a_k†(0)]> with Fourier exp(izt). Both frequency signs are required, regardless of whether a signed transition energy is positive. The leading large-z coefficient is1/z; the next is (sum Wplus*d_plus+sum Wminus*d_minus)/z². Analyticity in the upper half-plane is distinct from passivity in a fixed sector that need not minimize the energy over all populations. No chemical potential is chosen to make either insertion stable.

Under H -> H+kappa*N, eigenvectors/weights and Delta_c do not change; mu_plus and mu_minus both increase by kappa. Addition d_plus increases by kappa and removal d_minus decreases by kappa, so G_shift(z)=G_base(z-kappa). A shift proportional to number is not a global identity shift across the three sectors.

## Independent operator moment and asymptotic bound

Write epsilon_k=-2*C*cos(k) and b_k=L^(-1/2)*sum exp(-ikx)*n_x*a_x. The exact commutator is [a_k,H]=epsilon_k*a_k+g*b_k. Since [n_x*a_x,a_y†]=2*delta_xy*n_x, the coefficient c1=<[[a_k,H],a_k†]>=epsilon_k+2*g*N/L equals M1plus+M1remove. This uses total fixed number, not a mean-field factorization. Under the number offset it increases by kappa.

Subtracting 1/z+c1/z² gives the exact remainder R=z^(-2)*[sum Wplus*d_plus²/(z-d_plus)-sum Wminus*d_minus²/(z+d_minus)]. Consequently |R|<=(M2plus+M2remove)/(|z|²*Im z) for Im z>0. The test checks this analytic truncation bound separately from solver roundoff; it is not a floating-point certificate. These identities were independently derived before accepting the implementation.

## BPR interpretation

This calculation concerns the stipulated closed quantum ring, not an open-system boundary-attractor theorem. Number conservation and unitary evolution do not themselves supply preparation or attraction to the source ground state. The general BPR research idea is boundary-constrained collective-state selection in systems with specified dynamics; this quantum construction is a candidate ingredient, not a derivation of all such dynamics from a boundary alone. No cross-scale universality, holographic bulk reconstruction, impedance-derived hypercharge or physically selected prime is inferred here.
