## Scope

Keep the complete unchanged unit-filled Bose rings and the verified source definitions. Each ring is a separate conditional model, not a shared particle spectrum assembled across unrelated systems. Common scaling (C,g,z)->s(C,g,z) may constrain dimensionless model observables; it cannot select a physical clock, energy scale, length or probe normalization without independent calibration.

No parameter fitting, new interactions, physical measurement data or empirical validation is introduced. A software/model-consistency check using synthetic held-out data remains distinct from testing nature. The required physical preparation and observation map are presently missing.

## Reviewed scaling and observation distinctions

The unchanged Hamiltonian obeys H(sC,sg)=sH(C,g) for s>0. Source degrees are density0, number current1 and local energy1; energy-transfer current instead has degree2 and is not the j in this contract. Eigenvectors/projectors are unchanged by common scaling, gaps scale by s, integrated Gram weights by s^(d_a+d_b), first moments by s^(d_a+d_b+1), and retarded kernels at scaled frequency by s^(d_a+d_b-1).

The gap indexing ambiguity was resolved before any evaluation: Delta2 counts multiplicity, rather than the distinct-level interpretation considered in initial reviewer assignments. In the free L3..5 controls the ±1 one-boson excitations give Delta2/Delta1=1. This is deliberately retained even though uninformative about a second distinct energy. A small E2-E1 splitting is not an unresolved E2-E0 gap and cannot trigger a replacement observable. Free m1 density and symmetric-energy normalized moments and joint kernel ratio are also1; these free identities are not adopted as interacting predictions.

For observed response M_ab(z)=r_a(z)c_b(z)chi_ab(z), nonzero multiplicative row/readout and column/source gains cancel pointwise in Q=M_rhoh M_hrho/(M_rhorho M_hh), including independently signed and frequency-dependent gains. Additive backgrounds, channel mixing, zeros and nonfactorizable pair gains do not satisfy this cancellation. Raw observed reciprocity or PSD is not invariant under arbitrary signed row/column gains. Across-frequency entry ratios require frequency-independent gain products or explicit calibration. Normalized spectral moments require a constant gain across all contributing spectral lines, not merely across the three sampled frequencies. A common clock cancels at z=i eta Delta1 only when the same clock measures the actual ground gap; a first visible source pole need not equal that gap.

The held-out value comes from the complete frozen model, not extrapolation of two arbitrary interacting samples. Reversing exactly one off-diagonal at eta2 sends Q to-Q when defined. Rejection still requires separation2|Q| from the frozen numerical tolerance; a zero/unresolved channel must report insufficient discrimination rather than switch cases.

## Mathematical and numerical contract for final review

### Exact identities and numerical units

For u_na=<n|O_a|0>, W_ab=sum_n conj(u_na)u_nb and M_ab=sum_n Delta_n conj(u_na)u_nb, sum all excited states with their actual gaps. W and M are full2x2 matrices, not only their diagonals. The retarded convention is chi_ab(z)=sum_n[W_n,ab/(z-Delta_n)-W_n,ba/(z+Delta_n)]. In degenerate excited spaces only complete contractions/projectors, not individual eigenvectors or per-state weights, are invariant. Spectral densities with Dirac deltas have an additional inverse-energy Jacobian. For Hermitian A, W_A=<AQA>, M_A=<A(H-E0)A>=< [A,[H,A]] >/2, and M_A/(Delta1 W_A)>=1 if W_A>0. For the real ring/standing sources, -chi(iy) is PSD and defined Q lies in[0,1]; numerical values, imaginary components and residuals are never clipped.

Use C as the sole numerical unit: Obar_a=O_a/C^d_a, delta_n=Delta_n/C, Wbar_ab=W_ab/C^(d_a+d_b), Mbar_ab=M_ab/C^(d_a+d_b+1), Kbar_ab=C^(1-d_a-d_b)chi_ab. Observable definitions still use Delta1. Frozen d=(0,1) for(rho,h). All scale comparisons use these dimensionless quantities. Raw physical and dimensionless matrices are retained. For free m1, W=(N/L)[[1,alpha],[alpha,alpha²]], alpha=-C(1+cos(2pi/L)); the active gap is4Csin²(pi/L)=Delta1. All six free observables equal1 analytically. This does not apply to improved energy, whose free Q=alpha²/(alpha²+beta²), beta=Csin(k)/2.

### Frozen heuristic availability rules

Let dimension d_H, tau=256 eps_float64 d_H, r_a=||u_a/C^d_a||2, e_a=tau||Obar_a||F. Frobenius is explicitly chosen, not spectral norm. A source proven dark by a supplied exact identity has status zero_source; a numerical small value never supplies that proof. Otherwise r_a<=e_a gives unresolved_source, including computed zero; a resolved source has status available_heuristic. Retain raw W/M regardless. A normalized moment requires a resolved ground gap and resolved source; calculate Mbar_aa/(delta1 Wbar_aa) using exact-input products/division. No extra numerator-resolution gate. The public m1 grid has no declared structural dark sources; private fixed fixtures exercise that status.

Both delta1 and delta2 must exceed resolution/C. A small E2-E1 splitting is reported separately (unresolved_excited_splitting or resolved_excited_splitting), never a prerequisite for Delta2/Delta1. No snapping/clustering. A failed inherited eigensystem construction makes the whole case numerical_unavailable; an explicitly unresolved gap in owned/private fixtures gives unresolved_gap to dependent observables.

For eta in(.5,1,2), y=eta delta1, gamma=2 resolution/C, freeze
U_ab=2(e_a r_b+e_b r_a+e_a e_b)/y + 2(1+eta)gamma(r_a+e_a)(r_b+e_b)/y².
The first term is amplitude sensitivity; the second is shifted-spectrum and gap-derived-frequency sensitivity. These are stipulated HEURISTIC proxies, not bounds on eigenpair/source assembly errors. Inherited resolution is not a certified error. No alternative entry-relative floor, eightfold margin, precision gate or post-control tuning is adopted.

Set N=Kbar01 Kbar10 and D=Kbar00 Kbar11. ED=|Kbar00|U11+|Kbar11|U00+U00U11 and EN=|Kbar01|U10+|Kbar10|U01+U01U10. Exact supplied-input D=0 gives zero_denominator; nonzero |D|<=ED gives unresolved_denominator. Otherwise Q=N/D is available_heuristic, even for a zero numerator. Report UQ=(EN+|Q|ED)/(|D|-ED) as a diagnostic only. Its conditional algebraic error-bound interpretation requires actual input error bounds, which are absent here. It does not widen comparison tolerance or certify accuracy. A declared dark source overrides Q with zero_source; an unresolved source overrides Q with unresolved_source. Raw kernel remains available. The ratio helper independently exercises denominator gates without source metadata.

Complex products/sums/divisions and component differences use rational arithmetic on supplied finite components, before checked final conversion. Do not materialize an underflowing/overflowing denominator product merely to divide it. Modulus/product gates must be scale-safe (rational squared-modulus comparisons or equivalent guarded arithmetic). Nonrepresentable nonzero returned components/proxies give numerical_unavailable, never zero. Exact cancellation of supplied components is allowed. This arithmetic does not certify source assembly/eigenpairs. Private helper inputs are bounded, numeric, finite and shape-validated; bool, strings, custom conversion objects and complex-to-real casts are rejected. No blanket clipping.
