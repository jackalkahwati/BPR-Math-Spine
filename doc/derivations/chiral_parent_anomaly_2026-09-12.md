## Derived formulas for independent verification

Write S2=sum_i x_i² and S4=sum_i x_i⁴ for the five Spin(10) Cartan curvature variables. For epsilon_i=+-1 with product epsilon=+1, weights are(1/2)*sum epsilon_i*x_i. The sum of a product of any proper nonempty subset of epsilon_i is zero; only the empty and all-five subsets survive. The latter cannot enter trace powers at most4. Equivalently, the character is16*[product cosh(x_i/2)+product sinh(x_i/2)], with the second term first appearing at polynomial degree5; the opposite parity reverses that term only. The sixteen weights give tr16(1)=16, tr16(f)=tr16(f³)=0, tr16(f²)=4S2 and tr16(f⁴)=3S2²-2S4. Consequently the full parent polynomial is

I8=s*[(3S2²-2S4)/24 + Q²X²S2 + (2/3)Q⁴X⁴ - p1*S2/12 - p1*Q²X²/3 + (7p1²-4p2)/360].

The coefficients separate pure Spin(10), mixed Spin(10)-U(1), pure U(1), mixed tangent-Spin(10), mixed tangent-U(1), and pure tangent contributions. In particular p2 has coefficient -s/90, nonzero for one supplied complex Weyl16 even when Q=0. This is an irreducible local gravitational anomaly witness; cancellation is not established by lower-dimensional Standard Model charge checks.
