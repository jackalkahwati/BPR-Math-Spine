## Model and mathematical scope

Keep the unshifted complete Bose Hamiltonian

`H = (g/2) sum_x n_x(n_x-1) - C sum_x(a†_(x+1) a_x + a†_x a_(x+1))`

on the periodic ring, integer L>=3, C>0, g>=0. Full number sectors have N>=0 and dimension binomial(L+N-1,N). No local occupation cutoff, hard-core substitution, Hamiltonian shift, physical volume or fitted scale is introduced. Number-sector populations remain conserved; bounds do not prepare or select a population.

## Independent all-population derivation

Let Nhat=sum_x n_x. On finite-particle vectors,

`V + 2 C Nhat = C sum_x (a_x-a_(x+1))† (a_x-a_(x+1)) >= 0`,

`sum_x n_x² = Nhat²/L + sum_x(n_x-Nhat/L)²`.

For g>0 set alpha=g/2+2C, Nstar=L alpha/g and B_L=L alpha²/(2g). The exact positive-square identity is

`H + B_L I = (g/(2L))(Nhat-Nstar I)² + (g/2)sum_x(n_x-Nhat/L)² + C sum_x(a_x-a_(x+1))†(a_x-a_(x+1))`.

Consequently

`H_N >= g N²/(2L) - (g/2+2C)N >= -L(g/2+2C)²/(2g)`.

Nstar minimizes the real quadratic lower-bound polynomial, not the physical Hamiltonian over integer populations. The constant is uniform in L at fixed g>0,C, not automatically when g tends to zero with L. Neutral-sector restriction preserves it without supplying a neutral vacuum selection.

For fixed finite L and g>0, define H as the self-adjoint direct sum of finite Hermitian H_N with domain `sum_N ||H_N psi_N||² < infinity`. The bounds `H_N >= gN²/(2L)-alpha N` and `||H_N|| <= gN(N-1)/2+2CN` give graph-norm equivalence at large N, hence D(H)=D(Nhat²). The closed semibounded form domain is D(Nhat), and finite-number-support vectors form an operator and form core. On this form domain,

`q_H[psi] >= (g/(2L))||Nhat psi||² - alpha <psi,Nhat psi> >= -B_L ||psi||²`.

For population superpositions the quadratic quantity is <Nhat²>, not an asserted equality with <Nhat>². No finite energy expectation is asserted for arbitrary Fock vectors. The positive-g domain identifications and semibounded form statement are not carried over to g=0.

## Stronger integer-balanced and variational bounds

Write N=La+r, 0<=r<L. Transferring a particle from occupation u to v with u>=v+2 lowers the interaction count by u-v-1. Therefore

`Dmin = L a(a-1)/2 + a r = (N²/L-N)/2 + r(L-r)/(2L)`.

Retain inherited `lower_bound = g Dmin - 2 C N`, stronger than the quadratic lower bound by `g r(L-r)/(2L)`. Preserve the named trial upper bounds and their exact minimum:

- `balanced_occupation = g Dmin`.
- `uniform_condensate = -2 C N + g N(N-1)/(2L)`.
- At N=L only, `unit_filling_two_state = -2 C²(g+4C)/((g+2C)²+2C²)`.

The pair expression is a total-sector energy, not an energy per site. It arises from the normalized trial proportional to Omega+sqrt(2)*s*p, with Omega the unit occupation vector, p one adjacent doublon-hole state and s=C/(g+2C). The two-state compression has diagonal0,g and off-diagonal -sqrt(2)C. No sum over translated pairs is silently substituted.

For rho=N/L and delta=r/L, `Dmin/L = [rho²-rho+delta(1-delta)]/2`. Divide every total bound by L to obtain fixed-density intensive envelopes. The uniform trial per site is `-2 C rho + (g/2)(rho²-rho/L)`; the balanced correction need not vanish at noninteger density. Exact rational densities require integer rho*L without rounding.

At unit filling,

`-2 C <= E0(L,L)/L <= min(0,-2 C+(g/2)(1-1/L))`.

The reported stronger upper bound also includes the inherited pair trial divided by L. All named values remain visible, not only a minimum. For the scalar controls at rho=1/2,1,2, respectively Dmin=0,0,L. Fixed-density upper/lower bounds of order L do not prove convergence, differentiability or an equation of state in the thermodynamic limit.
