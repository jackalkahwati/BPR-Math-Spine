# BPR whole-theory audit: what survives and what must change

Date: 2026-09-12. Reviewed checkout: `main`, HEAD `7066f55`.

**Conclusion:** the repository does not presently derive a theory of everything. It contains useful mathematical models, conditional effective-field-theory calculations, numerical ansätze, and documented negative results. The missing connections are structural, not just missing numerical precision. A defensible continuation needs a new, explicit theoretical revision.

Companion: [Constructive extension and derivations](toe_constructive_extension_2026-09-12.md). Reproduce the independent algebra with `python3 scripts/check_toe_derivations.py`.

## 1. The original phase field is not an electromagnetic gauge field

**Sources:** `bpr/direct_coupling/gauge_symmetry.py:7-10,107-220`; `doc/BPR_Complete_Framework.md:382-413`.

The potential is

\[
H_V=J\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}[1-\cos(\theta_j-\theta_i)].
\]

It is invariant under a common shift. For finite clock variables the exact microscopic shift group is discrete; a continuous U(1) belongs to a rotor/continuum extension. Even in that extension the symmetry is **global**. A local shift changes a bond by

\[
\delta H_{ij}=J[\cos(\Delta\theta)-\cos(\Delta\theta+\Delta\alpha)]
=J\sin(\Delta\theta)\Delta\alpha
+\frac J2\cos(\Delta\theta)(\Delta\alpha)^2+\cdots.
\]

There is generally a linear term, not only the asserted gradient-squared term. At two equal phases, shifting one site by pi changes the energy by 2J. The repo's local test calls this a success: it explicitly requires the energy to change and only checks that both the change and a gradient sum are nonzero.

Moreover, phase-derived links satisfy

\[
U_{ij}=e^{i(\theta_j-\theta_i)},\qquad \prod_{\partial p}U_{ij}=1.
\]

Thus there is no independent plaquette curvature. Singular compact-phase configurations require separate treatment but do not turn a smooth pure-gradient connection into an independent Maxwell field.

**What survives:** global symmetry, a scalar/rotor model, conditional long-wavelength collective modes.

**What is needed:** independent link variables, their electric and magnetic dynamics, Gauss constraints, and a demonstrated relevant phase. Gauging is a new model, not a relabeling proof.

A related normalization check: for a hypercubic harmonic rotor with inertia I, nearest-neighbor bond energy J, and spacing a,

\[
\omega^2(\mathbf k)=\frac{2J}{I}\sum_{\mu=1}^{d}[1-\cos(k_\mu a)]
\longrightarrow \frac{Ja^2}{I}|\mathbf k|^2.
\]

Coordination does not automatically multiply this by z as asserted in `doc/BPR_Complete_Framework.md:424-433`. The conversion between q and theta, inertia, bond counting, and stiffness units must be fixed before comparing formulas. The repo's coarse-graining residual routine is a numerical diagnostic, not a continuum-limit proof.

## 2. Path B's frozen electric operator is not gauge invariant

**Sources:** `bpr/gauge_dynamics_m2_m3.py:35-54,143-170`; `tests/test_gauge_dynamics_m2_m3.py:80-110`.

Let L_g be left multiplication on the link Hilbert space C[G]. The stated generating-set Laplacian is

\[
\Delta_S=3I-L_r-L_{r^{-1}}-L_s,\quad G=D_n.
\]

Link endpoint gauge transformations act by left and right multiplication. The right action commutes with this operator, but the left action requires conjugacy-invariant weights. Since

\[
rsr^{-1}=r^2s\notin\{r,r^{-1},s\},
\]

that condition fails for every allowed n. The independent regular-representation check gives max absolute entry of `[Delta_S,L_r]` equal to one for n=5,8,9,12. This is not a floating-point residual.

In E_k, writing theta=2 pi k/n,

\[
\Delta_{E_k}=(3-2\cos\theta)I-\rho(s),
\]

whose eigenvalues are

\[
\boxed{2-2\cos\theta,\quad4-2\cos\theta}.
\]

The implemented `3-2 cos(theta)` is a trace average, not a scalar Casimir. In D5/E1 the two actual values are 1.381966 and 3.381966, not the quoted 2.381966. These are mathematical eigenvalues of a defective gauge Hamiltonian, not physical particle masses.

**Constructive result:** the conjugacy-twirled operator is positive, gauge invariant, and has the quoted trace averages as scalar eigenvalues. Its explicit form and compatible heat kernel are derived in the companion note. Adopting it changes the frozen model and must be recorded as a new proposal.

## 3. The simulated Wilson action is a different, valid gauge model

**Sources:** `bpr/gauge_phase_mc.py:3-23,61-78`; `bpr/gauge_mc_fast.py:5-10,26-73`.

The Euclidean character action is gauge invariant. Its temporal transfer kernel, normalized to have trivial-sector eigenvalue one, is

\[
w_\beta(g)=e^{\beta\chi_{E_1}(g)/2},\quad
K_\beta=\frac{\sum_gw_\beta(g)L_g}{\sum_gw_\beta(g)},
\]
\[
\tau_R=\frac{\sum_gw_\beta(g)\chi_R(g)^*}{d_R\sum_gw_\beta(g)},\quad
E_R=-\frac{\log\tau_R}{a_t}.
\]

At D5, beta_t=1.8 and a_t=1, the electric energies for A2,E1,E2 are approximately (1.098073,0.798723,1.492894). They are not proportional to the stated frozen spectrum (2,2.381966,4.618034), even after centralizing the latter. These are **single-link transfer energies**, not benchmark glueball masses.

The physical transfer matrix additionally contains the spatial potential and Gauss projector:

\[
T=P_G e^{-\beta_sV/2}(\otimes_\ell K_{\beta_t})e^{-\beta_sV/2}P_G.
\]

At finite time spacing, `-log(T)/a_t` includes noncommuting electric/magnetic contributions. A scalar beta-to-lambda calibration cannot repair an operator mismatch.

As beta_t grows, rotation and reflection rates scale differently. Their ratios to the identity weight are exp[-beta_t(1-cos(2 pi/n))] and exp[-beta_t], respectively. For these n, rotations dominate. A rescaling that retains their rate suppresses reflections, rather than producing the asserted equal-weight generator.

**Implication:** preserve existing simulations as Wilson-model calculations. Do not describe them as numerical evaluation of the frozen Hamiltonian without changing and documenting the model.

## 4. Internal representations do not determine physical spin/parity

**Sources:** `doc/BPR2_PATH_B_NONABELIAN_GAUGE.md:53-93`; `bpr/gauge_mc_fast.py:203-267`.

A2(D_n) is odd under an internal gauge reflection. It is not automatically odd under coordinate reflection, nor a local gauge-singlet glueball. Pure electric charges of the untwisted quantum double have topological spin one, including nontrivial internal irreps. E_k internal rotation weights likewise do not establish spacetime angular momentum.

The corrected fast code instead projects gauge-invariant loop shapes into **spatial C4v** channels. Those operators are useful. Independent random-configuration checks found gauge invariance to about 1e-16 and the intended nonzero parity-odd A2 response. A2(C4v) and A2(D_n) are different representations with the same conventional label.

The simulation is 2+1-dimensional on periodic spatial slices, not 3+1-dimensional QCD or an S2 simulation. C4v channels mix continuum angular momenta; rotational restoration and appropriate complementary channels must be established. Matching a few ratios would not prove the missing dimensional bridge.

The common leading-order `4 epsilon_min/lambda` claim also overreaches. That is an elementary four-link loop energy; the parity-odd and B1 projections of an elementary square vanish for the relevant real characters. One must minimize energy inside each **nonempty projected channel**, not assign the shortest loop to every channel.

## 5. M4 withholding rules are useful but not complete physics-validation gates

**Sources:** `bpr/gauge_mc_fast.py:311-402`; `doc/derivations/m4_compute_run_2026-09.md`.

The code appropriately withholds ratios when its checks fail. Before accepting future masses, it also needs:

- Connected-correlator vacuum subtraction recomputed in every resampling replica, not frozen at the full-sample estimate.
- Periodic-time correlator fits, covariance-aware uncertainties, fit-window and t0 stability, and excited-state control; two adjacent compatible slopes, potentially beginning at t=0, are insufficient.
- Finite-volume checks for every channel and verified matched partner metadata.
- A specified action/continuum trajectory. Mass ratios cancel a common time-unit conversion, not coupling-dependent spectral distortions or cutoff effects.
- Finite-size scaling, hysteresis/histograms, and autocorrelation analysis before deciding transition order. Sharp jumps on one small volume do not settle that question.

A finite first-order transition would obstruct the usual continuous-critical-point route to an arbitrarily large correlation length. It does not prove every other possible limit of an enlarged model impossible.

## 6. Flavor formulas survive as phenomenology, not derived assignments

**Sources:** `bpr/flavor_foundations.py`; `doc/derivations/color_bundle_index.md`; `doc/derivations/generations_from_CFT.md`; `doc/derivations/flavor_label_scan_2026-09.md`.

The September corrections are mathematically important:

- Ordinary SU(N) bundles on S2 have trivial determinant, so the relevant first-Chern Dirac index is zero.
- An added U(1) twist O(q) yields nonzero index, but q is an additional input. An index does not add a constant to an angular momentum label.
- Untwisted compact-boson conformal spin mn is integral. Counting spatial l=1 harmonics is not a derivation of fermionic families.
- The recent frozen-operator scan is negative, not evidence for the assigned labels.

M3's `spectrum_partition_check` merely partitions an unchanged list by residues. It is an exact identity but not a calculation of interacting gauged masses, their self-energies, or bound states.

Mass/mixing formulas need a renormalization scale, scheme, input ledger, and actual Yukawa operators. Formula agreement at mixed mass conventions cannot replace those. The constructive note gives a genuine index calculation and demonstrates why it still does not predict three families, hierarchy, or mixing.

Additional concrete checks:

- `qcd_flavor.py:301-405` inserts a CKM phase, calculates its Jarlskog invariant, then reconstructs the phase from that invariant. This is an identity, not an independent phase derivation. Default constructors also retain measured mass/mixing inputs; supplying the EW scale without p still fits the down mass in the branch at line 243.
- `charged_leptons.py:25-135` gives a mass-derived Koide value about 0.665349, not the separately asserted exact 2/3. An approximate ansatz and an exact identity are different claims.
- `neutrino.py:30-192` modifies the reported solar mass splitting without modifying its returned masses. Those outputs do not describe a single consistent spectrum. Its PMNS coefficients include acknowledged fits.
- `qcd_flavor.py:424-450` does not solve strong CP: orientability does not force the integral of F wedge F to vanish, and physical theta-bar includes arg det(M_u M_d). An orientable four-sphere can support nonzero instanton number.
- Zero winding or center charge does not guarantee a non-Abelian singlet or demonstrate confinement. Anchored heavy-baryon spin splittings can remain falsifiable ansätze, but heavy-quark symmetry alone does not fix every light-quark mass response or the transferred coupling coefficient.

## 7. Alpha and GUT agreement contain inverse calibration and unproved response maps

**Sources:** `bpr/alpha_derivation.py` (`prime_for_exact_alpha`); `bpr/cs_completion.py:359-404,478-518,629-707`; `bpr/gauge_unification.py:24-30,554-563,617-654`; `doc/derivations/p_selection_principle.md`.

Selecting a nearby prime by inverting observed alpha is an experimental anchor. It does not independently explain alpha. The current expression evaluated at p=104761 gives inverse alpha about 137.0386409; older module prose gives different arithmetic. This agreement is a conditional numerical property, not evidence that p is independently selected.

The U(1) Chern-Simons topological entropy calculation does not by itself establish `(ln p)^2` as a four-dimensional electromagnetic kinetic coefficient. The implementation defines the proposed response and checks the same definition. A basic source-integrated CS theory produces a 2+1D topological Hall/CS response, not automatically a 4D Maxwell coefficient. The Hopf base S2 is not the boundary of the closed S3 total space; an edge construction must be specified.

GUT backward matching is algebraically fitted. If run-up inverse couplings are u_i and a common value U is chosen, setting delta_i=U-u_i and subtracting those same deltas on running down reproduces the input couplings identically. Calling the result conditional does not make it an independent prediction.

Forward threshold coefficients require actual gauge representations, masses, and normalized current couplings. Site-overlap counting alone does not determine beta-function contributions. `gut_winding_spectrum.py:205-210` appropriately labels assigning compact-boson states to X,Y bosons an ansatz. Its closure condition checks correction magnitude rather than the corrected residual; applying its stated corrections to its stated forward values actually increases the pairwise inverse-coupling spread, approximately 0.440 to 1.174.

For a simultaneous unitary holomorphic affine-current realization, commuting SU(3),SU(2),U(1) currents need central charge at least four. The original c=1 boundary theory cannot furnish that particular realization. This is a precise conditional obstruction, not a universal no-go theorem against all emergent constructions.

## 8. The historical E8 assignment is not a branching calculation

**Source:** `bpr/clifford_bpr.py:300-385`.

The E8 root enumeration is useful mathematics. `e8_to_sm_decomposition`, however, assigns consecutive root indices and manually specified particle charges. It does not calculate subgroup generators, their eigenweights, branching multiplicities, spin/statistics, or chirality.

The displayed E6 claim `27 -> 10 + 5bar + 1` loses eleven dimensions. The correct Spin(10) decomposition is 27=16+10+1; under SU(5), 16=10+5bar+1 and 10=5+5bar. The omitted states matter. The E8 adjoint branching into (27,3) and its conjugate also does not remove mirror chirality by itself. Three families is explicitly returned as a constant.

A subgroup embedding is not a dynamical explanation of chiral matter. A constructive smaller Spin(10) target is set out in the companion, without calling its field content derived.

## 9. Quantum mechanics and the Born rule are assumed in key steps

**Sources:** `bpr/born_rule.py:133-152`; `bpr/zp_ergodicity.py:11-35,64-72,209-218`; `bpr/symbolic_derivations.py:151-195`; `doc/conjectures/born_rule.md` appended closure claims.

The Born sampler allocates sites proportional to target squared amplitudes and then samples with probabilities proportional to squared random amplitudes. These are assumptions of the rule being explained.

The valid independent-phase identity E|sum exp(i theta_j)|^2=n does not imply that the expectation of a normalized intensity equals the ratio of expected intensities. Exact enumeration for p=3 with two and four sites gives approximately 0.397861 for the first normalized-intensity average, not 1/3. A one-site floor also prevents exact zero-probability nodes in that construction.

A finite bijection preserves uniform measure but cannot attract every initial fine-grained measure to it. A delta distribution at a fixed point is a counterexample. Uniform marginal distributions do not imply independence.

The symbolic quantum calculation starts from a Schrodinger action containing i hbar psi* d_t psi. Recovering its equations is conditional consistency, not derivation of quantum theory from a classical finite substrate. A conservative continuation may **postulate quantum mechanics explicitly**; a stronger deterministic emergence claim must supply independent preparation, measurement dynamics, and probability measure.

## 10. Gravity is inserted before several purported derivations

**Sources:** `bpr/boundary_action.py:61-81`; `bpr/symbolic_derivations.py:282-370`; `paper/textbook/main.tex:1813-1823`; `doc/derivations/graviton_propagator_from_boundary_tt.md`.

The textbook explicitly adds the boundary term to Einstein-Hilbert plus Standard Model actions. The symbolic routine directly constructs the linearized Einstein equation rather than obtaining it from the displayed variational calculation.

A correct transverse-traceless projector is not evidence for a dynamical graviton pole. A spatial kernel P_TT/(M^2 k^2), conditional on Einstein-Hilbert, does not establish the full Lorentzian pole, universal coupling, or gravitational constraint algebra. The latest gravity note appropriately leaves important parts of that map open.

The proposed induced-gravity coefficient further assumes that p primary/anyon sectors of one c=1 theory are p independent four-dimensional scalar fields. That field-content identification is not derived. Heat-kernel formulas become applicable only after specifying actual bulk fields, couplings, regulator, and counterterms.

**What survives:** conditional GR-plus-boundary-matter EFT calculations, the TT projector, and standard induced-action techniques.

## 11. Planck and scalaron normalizations require correction

**Sources:** `doc/derivations/planck_length_from_substrate.md:48-76,132-136`; `doc/derivations/horizon_entropy_from_induced_gravity.md:23-37`; `bpr/emergent_spacetime.py:187-274`; `bpr/graviton_propagator.py:128-169`; `doc/derivations/scalaron_sector_from_boundary_r2.md`.

For S_EH=(M^2/2) integral sqrt(-g) R, M is the **reduced** Planck mass: M^2=1/(8 pi G) in natural units. Inserting the unreduced mass sqrt(1/G) changes the normalization by 8 pi. Wald entropy for that coefficient is 2 pi A M^2, not A M^2/4.

For the displayed action

\[
S=\int\sqrt{-g}\left[\frac{M^2}{2}R+\frac{\alpha}{2}R^2\right],
\]

the correct results are

\[
V(\varphi)=\frac{M^4}{8\alpha}(1-e^{-\sqrt{2/3}\varphi/M})^2,
\quad m_\varphi^2=\frac{M^2}{6\alpha},
\quad A_s\simeq\frac{N_e^2}{144\pi^2\alpha}.
\]

The repo uses different factors. Correcting them changes its required amplitude enhancement from about 7.98 million to 5.32 million for the quoted inputs. The proposed near-match based on the former value no longer follows. Even the corrected calculation remains conditional on the field content, loop coefficient, e-fold prescription, and reheating history.

## 12. Neither delta=2 nor the MEMS amplitude is established

**Sources:** `bpr/recursive_boundary.py:179-200,687-723`; `bpr/phason_sector.py:186-195,245-254`; `bpr/casimir.py:93-112,271-291`; `bpr/direct_coupling/em_casimir_corrected.py:55-77`; `bpr/direct_coupling/collective_modes.py:250-315,521-575`.

For a Pisot unit, the norm fixes the product of contracting conjugates, not each contraction. The ninefold polynomial x^3-3x^2+1 has geometric weights approximately 0.403405 and 0.596595, summing to one. The newer volume-product argument still assumes, rather than derives, how a physical field transforms. Geometry alone does not fix its scaling dimension, the boundary operator dimension, or a Casimir force exponent.

Rotational symmetry permits all representation sectors. The code does contain an extra invariant-field penalty: that is a separate state-selection postulate, not a consequence of geometry, and is a strict restriction only in its hard-constraint limit.

The Casimir routine computes a sourced classical field energy and inserts the proposed power. It does not evaluate a quantum interaction determinant. It also varies plate area with the length used as separation, unlike a fixed-area force experiment.

The displayed effective electric field `(hbar c/lambda)*|grad phi|` has SI units of force, not V/m. A physical charge/coupling is missing. Resulting squared ratios and signal magnitudes cannot be used as dimensionless predictions.

For a mechanical oscillator,

\[
\chi_x(\Omega)=\frac1{k-m\Omega^2-i m\Gamma\Omega},\quad
\frac{\delta f}{f}=\frac12\left(\frac{\delta k}{k}-\frac{\delta m}{m}\right).
\]

Q amplifies driven displacement near resonance; it does not automatically multiply an intrinsic frequency shift. N-squared enhancement also requires specified coherence, normalization, energy budget, and detector mode. The advertised enhancement stack does not provide that transduction calculation.

## 13. Cosmology and dark matter have independent unresolved failures

**Sources:** `bpr/impedance.py:77-123,167-264,462-553`; `bpr/cosmology.py:49-101,473-706,771-895`; `bpr/jwst_cosmology.py:438-526,841-846`; `bpr/bridges/cosmology_gravity.py:550-640`; `experiments/dark_sector_comparison.py`.

- The proposed dark-energy density uses Planck mass squared in kg^2 divided by area, not a physical density. A bridge compares incompatible units and claims agreement that its raw arithmetic does not produce. A separate version explicitly normalizes to observed H0 and Omega_Lambda. Neither solves the vacuum-energy or radiative-stability problem.
- The assumed phonon rate Gamma proportional T yields Gamma/H proportional 1/T in radiation domination: cooling causes **recoupling**, not the stated early decoupling. The additional-radiation expression also uses the wrong entropy/temperature normalization. A genuinely early-decoupled real scalar with ordinary SM entropy history gives the illustrative Delta_Neff about 0.0268, not the advertised 0.00568; the proposed rate does not establish even that history.
- The relic formula gives Omega h^2 about 0.1197 with Wc=sqrt(3), but about 4.065 with its alternative unified Wc=p^(1/5). The comparison code usefully exposes this overproduction. Multiplicative coannihilation factors are not a substitute for normalized effective cross sections and Boltzmann evolution.
- One MOND function returns a_N+a0 rather than the deep-MOND limit. A separate rotation-curve implementation correctly solves the assumed interpolation and recovers v^4=GMa0. Neither supplies a covariant lensing/growth theory.
- If a0 is proportional to H at every epoch as claimed, substituting it in the proposed cosmological transition condition cancels H. The quoted transition redshift results from freezing a0 today while evolving H, an incompatible assumption.
- Inflation uses an observation-normalized scalar amplitude, an inconsistent epsilon-versus-r factor, and a reheating estimate exceeding the energy available under its own standard inflationary normalization. Dark-energy EOS variants disagree and are not evolved from a common action with perturbative stability tests.
- JWST comparisons contain observed cosmological normalization, astrophysical modeling, and successive discretionary prescriptions. They are useful comparisons, not zero-input cosmological derivations.

A successful particle model would not automatically fix any of these. One must evolve a common, conserved model through reheating, BBN, recombination, structure formation, and late acceleration.

## What can be retained

1. Exact finite-group and compact-boson calculations, with correct domains and interpretations.
2. Corrected Wilson-loop operators and the practice of withholding unsupported spectral ratios.
3. The conditional monopole index, explicitly distinguishing internal spin from spacetime spin and chosen flux from predicted family count.
4. Conditional standard EFT calculations, including TT projection and familiar cosmological limits, without calling their assumed actions derived.
5. Flavor formulae as frozen phenomenological hypotheses with a transparent input/selection history.
6. Negative findings as constraints on new proposals, not obstacles to be erased.
