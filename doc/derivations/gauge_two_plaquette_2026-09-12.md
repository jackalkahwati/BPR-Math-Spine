## Frozen scope and controls

Two adjacent oriented squares have six vertices and seven links. A five-edge spanning tree leaves two based face holonomies. Reduce by residual simultaneous conjugation with an orbit-normalized basis in counting measure. Derive all seven link electric actions from canonical graph representatives, not a tensor sum of isolated squares. The shared edge couples the two faces and must preserve the outer-loop product with compatible face orientations. Check every vertex gauge action independently.

Groups D5,D8,D9 have orders10,16,18 and unreduced two-holonomy coordinate dimensions100,256,324. Use the existing central reflection conjugacy class, including its existing even-n convention, and the inherited rotation terms. Coupling lambda=1.3 and symmetric transfer steps .08,.04,.02 are fixed before evaluation. Each face has the existing potential1-chi_E1/2. No new matter, bath, fitted couplings, physical scale or production simulation is introduced.

The inherited single-link generator is Delta=3I-L_r-L_(r^-1)-|C_s|^-1*sum_(g in C_s)L_g, where C_s is the conjugacy class of s=(0,-1). For even n this contains only even-index reflections, not both reflection classes. Group elements use (k,eps), r^k times identity/reflection, in rotations-then-reflections ordering. Regular action matrices act on columns. Existing heat kernels are probability mass in counting measure, not densities against normalized Haar measure. The inherited square convention is electric4*Delta/lambda plus lambda*(1-chi_E1/2); the new magnetic sum must preserve this normalization.

No seven-link tensor product is allocated. Cap two-holonomy space at324 before enumeration/allocation; old effective_hamiltonian's cap24 remains unchanged. Unsupported inputs/scales raise rather than fabricate results. Analytic transfer positivity/refinement statements are distinct from floating eigensolver/residual diagnostics.

## Frozen graph convention

Vertices are A (top middle), B (bottom middle), Ltop,Lbot,Rbot,Rtop. Oriented edges are a:A->Ltop, b:Ltop->Lbot, c:Lbot->B, d:A->B (shared), e:B->Rbot, f:Rbot->Rtop, h:Rtop->A. Root is A; tree is (a,b,d,e,f), leaving c,h. Based face holonomies are u=a*b*c*d^-1 and v=d*e*f*h, so uv is the outer loop. The tree slice sets all tree links to identity with c=u and h=v.

Left translation of one link by g on this slice gives (g*u,v) for a,b,c; (u*g^-1,g*v) for d; and (u,g*v) for e,f,h. The shared action preserves uv exactly. Gauge transformations act as U_(x,y)->k_x*U_(x,y)*k_y^-1. Tree fixing with root k_A=z uses k_Ltop=z*a, k_Lbot=z*a*b, k_B=z*d, k_Rbot=z*d*e, k_Rtop=z*d*e*f. The remaining links become c'=z*u*z^-1 and h'=z*v*z^-1. Taking z=e fixes the tree but retains the residual conjugation action as the physical quotient. Off this slice the translation element is conjugated by the appropriate path prefix. Thus an individual fixed-g action is not claimed to descend as a gauge-invariant operator; the central convolution does. This distinction is part of the independent graph/gauge tests.

For a gauge element k at one vertex, the changed links are: A: a->k*a,d->k*d,h->h*k^-1; B: c->c*k^-1,d->d*k^-1,e->k*e; Ltop: a->a*k^-1,b->k*b; Lbot: b->b*k^-1,c->k*c; Rbot: e->e*k^-1,f->k*f; Rtop: f->f*k^-1,h->k*h. Direct cancellation leaves both holonomies invariant at nonroot vertices and conjugates both at A. Before tree fixing, left link translations insert conjugated g with prefixes identity,a,ab on a,b,c and d,de,def on e,f,h. The shared link d acts as(u*g^-1,g*v) even before fixing. Centrality permits reindexing the weighted insertion; it does not make fixed-g translations physical observables.

The genuine single-square graph control deletes the entire right three-edge path. Its four links give3Delta_left+Delta_right=4Delta on the remaining class functions. Merely turning off the right magnetic potential leaves two loops and is not that graph reduction. As a separate operator check within the two-face space, functions of u alone or v alone see4Delta, since the opposite exterior paths annihilate them. Deleting both non-tree edges c,h leaves a tree with one physical state,H=0,T=1. Products of separate face characters do not span the full simultaneous-conjugation invariant space.

## Orbit normalization and analytical counts

Write q=|G|. Residual gauge action is simultaneous conjugation on G². For each orbit O the counting-measure embedding is S_(x,O)=1/sqrt(|O|) for x in O, zero otherwise. Thus S†S=I and SS† is the simultaneous-conjugation projector. In normalized Haar measure on G² the corresponding function is q/sqrt(|O|) times the orbit indicator; raw representatives without orbit weights are not an orthonormal basis. Burnside gives d=(1/q)*sum_g |C_G(g)|²=sum_conjugacy_classes |C_G(rep)|. For odd n, d=(n²+3n+4)/2; for even n, d=n²/2+3n+8. The resulting analytical counts are22,64,56 for D5,D8,D9 respectively. D12 is excluded by its576-dimensional pair space, irrespective of the smaller physical quotient.

Let Delta_u,Delta_v,Delta_shared denote the same inherited weighted central generator applied to the three kinds of coordinate action above. The continuous electric operator is E=(3Delta_u+3Delta_v+Delta_shared)/lambda, compressed by S. The unscaled magnetic coordinate diagonal is M(u,v)=2-chi_E1(u)/2-chi_E1(v)/2; physical H=E+lambda*M. The shared term is retained, not replaced by another pair of independent generators.

All three electric pieces commute on the full pair space. The implemented transfer is required to use inherited central heat-kernel factors, compressed individually to the physical invariant space before multiplication, rather than exponentiating an eigensolver's rounded electric zero mode. This is a structural numerical choice made before frozen spectral evaluation; positivity and resolution screens remain necessary. In the shared representation S_g=R_g on u times L_g on v, Delta_u commutes with the right action, while Delta_v is central in the left action and commutes with each L_g. Consequently exp(-tE) equals the product of central heat convolutions on u,v,shared with times3t,3t,t and the same lambda. The shared heat convolution preserves uv. This provides a structural implementation independent of electric eigenvectors and an oracle against a direct matrix exponential; no commutation with the magnetic potential is assumed.

## Transfer bounds to verify independently

Put A=E, B=lambda*M on the physical space, both positive semidefinite, and H=A+B. Use T(t)=exp(-tB/2)exp(-tA)exp(-tB/2). If a=||A||, b=||B|| and Ccomm=||[A,[A,B]]||/12+||[B,[A,B]]||/24, the contraction-semigroup Strang bound is ||T(t)-exp(-tH)||<=t³*Ccomm. Both positive matrices have minimum eigenvalue at least exp[-t(a+b)]. The integral representation of log then gives ||-log(T(t))/t-H||<=t²*exp[t(a+b)]*Ccomm. For the error proof set X=-B,Y=-A and S(t)=exp(tX/2)exp(tY)exp(tX/2). Write P(t)=exp(tX/2), Q(t)=exp(tY), K=[X,Y]. The defect has the exact expression D(t)=(1/2)*integral_0^t {P(t-s)[K,P(s)]Q(t)+P(t)[K,Q(t-s)]Q(s)} ds *P(t). Commutator Duhamel formulas give ||[K,P(s)]||<=s*||[K,X]||/2 and ||[K,Q(t-s)]||<=(t-s)*||[K,Y]||. Thus in the defect S'-(X+Y)S, first-commutator terms cancel. Expanding the remaining commutators as integrals of contraction semigroups gives norm at most t²*||[X,[X,Y]]||/8+t²*||[Y,[X,Y]]||/4. Duhamel integration from0 to t yields the constants1/24 and1/12 above. For positive P,Q with both minimum eigenvalues at least m, the resolvent integral for log gives ||log(P)-log(Q)||<=||P-Q||*integral_0^infinity (m+s)^-2 ds=||P-Q||/m. Taking m=exp[-t(a+b)] proves the stated negative-log bound. These are analytical operator-norm bounds; substituting floating norms gives diagnostics rather than certified numerical enclosures. The formal leading generator correction is t²*([A,[A,B]]/12+[B,[A,B]]/24), with remainder O(t⁴). A finite-step observed ratio is not the proof of this expansion. Each inherited Delta is bounded by6I, hence a<=42/lambda and b<=4lambda. Nested-commutator norm inequalities give Ccomm<=a²*b/3+a*b²/6. These a priori constants do not require evaluating the frozen spectra. Between steps t and s, the generator difference is bounded by Ccomm*(t²*exp(t*(a+b))+s²*exp(s*(a+b))). For s=t/2 this is a refinement bound, not a guaranteed observed ratio4. The leading coefficient scales as lambda^-1 and lambda for fixed unscaled electric and magnetic operators; this is an algebraic consequence, not a fitted scaling law.

## Frozen API

Public graph functions are canonical_links(n,u,v), holonomies(n,links), gauge_transform(n,links,vertex_values), tree_fix(n,links), link_action(n,edge,g,u,v) and edge_laplacian(n,edge). tree_fix returns links and vertex_values with root identity. edge_laplacian is the unscaled coordinate central generator. Pair order is the Cartesian product of inherited elements order; discover each orbit at the earliest unvisited row and sort its row indices. orbit_basis returns n,elements,pairs,orbits,basis,count,burnside_count.

model(n,lam=1.3) returns orbit metadata, model_id,lam,coordinate_dimension,physical_dimension,reflection_class,laplacian_u,laplacian_v,laplacian_shared (unscaled), coordinate_electric,coordinate_magnetic,coordinate_hamiltonian and physical electric,magnetic,hamiltonian,magnetic_diagonal. Electric matrices already include1/lam; magnetic matrices are unscaled and H=electric+lam*magnetic. transfer(model,dt) returns symmetric transfer. The new effective_hamiltonian(T,dt) accepts a bounded Hermitian positive-definite matrix and returns its negative log over dt; it does not assume every generic input is a contraction or force its logarithm positive. diagnostics(n,lam,steps) reports raw spectra, resolution/conditioning, exact-exponential errors, commutator constants, analytic-bound evaluations and step refinement. demonstration_report uses only frozen controls. Public boundaries validate group/domain/shape before allocation, including zero-time branches.
