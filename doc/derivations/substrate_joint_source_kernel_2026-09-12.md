## Reviewed spectral and static conventions

For one complete eigensystem and a common ground |0>, set u_na=<n|O_a|0>, Delta_n=E_n-E0 and W^(n)_ab=conj(u_na)u_nb for n>0. Each exact degenerate-projector sum is positive semidefinite, independently of eigenbasis choice. A grouped numerical Gram sum does not certify exact degeneracy. Retain every actual gap in chi_ab(z)=sum_n[W_ab/(z-Delta_n)-W_ba/(z+Delta_n)] and K_ab=-2 Re sum_n W_ab/Delta_n. Positivity of W does not imply positivity of chi. The negative-frequency numerator is transposed. Complex two-level test sources are necessary to catch sign/conjugation errors that real ring sources cannot detect: H=diag(0,delta), O1=sigma_x,O2=sigma_y gives W12=i, chi12=i/(z-delta)+i/(z+delta), chi21=-chi12 and K12=0. Reciprocity diagnostics apply only to the real zero-flux ring with real standing sources, not arbitrary Hermitian fixtures. Independent full-space resolvent contractions and the reduced solve H-E0+P0 test response/static formulas without reusing Lehmann helpers. Exact-degenerate unitary-rotation fixtures test summed Gram invariance; unequal-gap states are never rotated together. These are test oracles, not an expanded scientific control grid.

For real O'=R O, W'=R W R^T, chi'=R chi R^T and K'=R K R^T; source coordinates obey f=R^T f'. Define physical source coordinates q=(C f_rho,f_h) and D=diag(C,1), so K_f=D K_q D. If energy is divided by C its Hessian is K_f/C; this module must state which output is used. The linear-family K is negative semidefinite. The fixed scalar contact adds C diag(1,0) to the dimensionless-coordinate energy Hessian without changing connected kernels or gaps, and the contact-shifted Hessian need not be negative semidefinite.

Standing waves use real cosine coefficients and real local source matrices, not separately rounded ±k Fourier operators. ## Independently reviewed finite-difference envelope

Let X=C O_rho and Y=O_h. For direction B define D_B(h)=[E0(hB)+E0(-hB)-2E0]/h². Retain axes B=X,Y and corners B=X+Y,X-Y at each fixed h. The mixed four-corner stencil is [D_(X+Y)(h)-D_(X-Y)(h)]/4. It uses all four signs and is not an axial approximation.

For exact Hermitian H with unique ground, a genuine lower gap bound delta>0 and genuine upper norm bound ||B||2<=b, the isolated eigenvalue branch is analytic for complex |t|<R=delta/(2b). Normal-matrix spectral inclusion puts every perturbed eigenvalue in the |t|b neighbourhood of the original spectrum; the contour |w-E0|=delta/2 has constant Riesz rank1. Thus |E(t)-E0|<=b|t|, and the analytic quotient [E(t)-E0]/t is bounded by b. Cauchy estimates at radii approaching R give |c_n|<=b/R^(n-1). Summing even terms n>=4 proves the adopted axial truncation envelope

Bax(b,h,delta)=16 b^4 h²/[delta³(1-(2bh/delta)²)], strictly2bh<delta.

For b=0 set Bax=0. The mixed envelope is [Bax(b_plus,h,delta)+Bax(b_minus,h,delta)]/4, provided both directional conditions hold. Independent static derivation and adversarial review confirm the factor16 and the complex-parameter branch argument. Alternative looser interior-radius bounds are not adopted. This choice preceded control evaluation. The quadratic scalar contact is exact in these stencils, with zero extra truncation remainder, and it leaves spectral isolation unchanged.

## Independently reviewed analytic model bounds

At unit filling the onsite D has a unique zero eigenvalue and next eigenvalue1. Min-max and ||K||<=2CN give E1>=g-2CN, while the occupation-state trial energy gives E0<=0; hence Delta>=g-2CN. Separately gD>=0 implies E1>=-2CN+4C sin²(pi/L), and the uniform-condensate trial gives E0<=-2CN+gN(N-1)/(2L). Adopt the fixed lower bound delta_model=max(0,4C sin²(pi/L)-gN(N-1)/(2L),g-2CN). Zero means this lower-bound method is unavailable, not that the actual gap vanishes. In particular the frozen L=N5,C1,g=.7 case has delta_weak=(5-sqrt5)/2-7/5<0 and delta_strong<0, so its model-bound method is analytically known to be unavailable before evaluation. Retain that result without a post-hoc alternate bound.

For every supported nonzero standing mode set wmax=sqrt(2/L). Adopt b_rho=C N wmax, b_h=wmax[g binom(N,2)+2CN], and b_plus=b_minus=b_rho+b_h. The density constant term sums to zero. The energy onsite diagonal is bounded by wmax g binom(N,2); each partition has nonnegative link weights summing to1, so the one-particle hopping-source row sum is at most2C wmax and its N-particle lift at most2CN wmax. These simple bounds are used unchanged for both partitions; no control-dependent sharpening. Both independent derivations and adversarial review accept the gap and norm bounds.

Their substitution into Bax is an analytic theorem for the exact stipulated operators with a floating, non-enclosed evaluation here, not a machine error certificate. Separately evaluate a heuristic envelope from computed gap and computed spectral norms of X,Y,X+Y,X-Y. Neither estimate covers eigensolver/source-assembly error. Preserve both methods and all unavailable cases.

## Independently confirmed free-source distinction

The independent spectral derivation distinguishes the free partitions rather than extending the symmetric relation by assumption. With the inherited Fourier sign and improved weights(1/4,3/4), it finds h_k|G>_conn=-C[1+cos(k)+(i/2)sin(k)]rho_k|G>_conn. With O_rho,s=(rho_k-rho_-k)/(i sqrt2), the real energy source acts as alpha O_rho,c+(C/2)sin(k)O_rho,s on the free ground, where alpha=-C(1+cos(k)). For distinct ±k the resulting Gram matrix is (N/L)*[[1,alpha],[alpha,alpha²+C²sin²(k)/4]], unlike the symmetric rank-one matrix. At the self-inverse k=pi control both energy sources are dark and the density standing-wave weight doubles to2N/L. The adversarial reviewer independently confirms these identities. At k=pi the symmetric free energy source is the zero operator, whereas the improved source may be nonzero as an operator while annihilating this particular ground state. These are analytical identities, not evaluated controls or a final API freeze.
