## Flux convention and parity

At fixed particle number N the Jordan-Wigner boundary phase is theta_N=0 for odd N and pi for even N. Put alpha=theta_N+phi and choose a one-body matrix with interior h[x,x+1]=-C, closing h[L-1,0]=-C exp(i alpha), and Hermitian conjugates. The eigenvectors have components exp(i k_m x)/sqrt(L), k_m=(2pi m+alpha)/L, with epsilon_m=-2C cos(k_m). This follows directly by applying the closing link to the last component. The N-body spectrum is the sum over N distinct occupied modes, not a sum with repeated levels.

At phi+2pi the closing matrix is identical and mode labels permute m->m+1 modulo L. This is a large-gauge spectral equivalence, not spectral creation of an unpaired chiral species. In a uniform-link gauge put G_xx=exp(i*alpha*x/L); then G†hG has every forward matrix element -C exp(i*alpha/L), including the closing link. Test the closing-link convention directly; do not combine incompatible phase conventions.

## Branches and signed crossings

For an interior reference mu in (-2C,2C), define k_F=acos(-mu/(2C)). The two continuum branches at +k_F and -k_F have opposite velocities v=d epsilon/dk=2C sin(k), and slopes with respect to flux v/L. A partial-filling reference mu=-2C cos(pi N/L) is a declared band-reference convention, not a selected population. It also has an exact finite-volume interpretation at zero flux: the parity-correct hard-core ground energy is E0(N)=-2C sin(pi N/L)/sin(pi/L), so [E0(N+1)-E0(N-1)]/2=mu for 0<N<L. This symmetric finite-difference chemical potential is generally distinct from the individual addition/removal energies and from a flux-dependent occupation cut. The neighboring sectors use their own parity boundary conditions. Empty and full controls have no two nonzero-velocity Fermi branches.

Over phi in [0,2pi), each of +k_F and -k_F modulo2pi is crossed once among all mode labels. Solve L*k_F_branch-theta_N=2pi*m+phi modulo 2pi L; keep two distinct events even if flux values tie. Crossing signs are +1 and -1, so net signed flow is zero. At mu=+-2C, the two branches coincide and give one tangency of sign0, not two transverse crossings. Outside the band there are no events. An event at phi=0 is included; the phi=2pi duplicate is excluded. An explicit construction is p=0 for odd N,1 for even N, beta_s=s*k_F, u_s=L*beta_s/(2pi)-p/2, j_s=floor(u_s), phi_s=2pi*(u_s-j_s), m_s=j_s modulo L, sign=s. Using signed beta rather than subtracting k_F from 2pi avoids a near-zero cancellation. At mu=0, use exact rational u_s=s*L/4-p/2 so endpoint ties remain structural. Exact special endpoints (mu=0 or band edges) should use structural angle values; floating-point tie classification must be labeled numerical and must not invent near-edge zero velocities.

Finite-volume ground filling can have degeneracies. An additional algebraic tie control is L=5,N=2,phi=pi: the parity-plus-flux phase is 2pi, so the lowest singlet is followed by a paired level split by the occupation cut, giving two Slater ground states. At phi=0 the lowest antiperiodic pair is filled completely and the ground is unique. Report exact numerical ordered levels, occupied-mode ground sums, gap at the occupation cut and degeneracy/tie diagnostics; do not silently choose a unique state through a Fermi-level tie. Opposite-velocity branches are a property of the band/reference, not proof of a gapless finite ring.

## Charge and neutrality

## Finite-g negative control

Reuse the existing complete-basis second-order correction, not a new interaction: for L=5,N=2, the adjacent-pair diagonal is -4C²/g and the correlated pair transfer |11000> to |01100> is -2C²/g. These selected entries alone do not exclude every possible quadratic one-body correction. The full diagonal supplies the sharper test: it is -4C²/g on adjacent pairs and zero on separated pairs. A number-conserving quadratic Hamiltonian in the inherited Jordan-Wigner variables has occupation-basis diagonal constant+sum_x d_x n_x. If it matched this translation-invariant pair diagonal, averaging its translates would leave the target unchanged but make every d_x equal; at fixed N the result would be constant, a contradiction.
