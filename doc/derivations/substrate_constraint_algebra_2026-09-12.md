## Reviewed identities and predetermined witnesses

For the connected ring at fixed N, move particles one at a time along a spanning path toward site0. Every move has nonzero occupation matrix element for C>0; all states connect to (N,0,...,0), including the singleton vacuum case. Thus a diagonal F commutes with H iff it is constant. The onsite interaction never changes this graph. Site occupation takes every integer0..N. An onsite residue projector is nontrivial precisely when its indicator varies on that interval; mod3 residue2 at N<2 is zero, not a failed local conservation witness. For N>=1 and q>=2, canonical residue r is nonconstant iff r<=N and otherwise zero; none is identity. A crossing hop for r0 begins with n_x=0,n_y=N and moves y to x, giving magnitude C sqrt(N). For r>=1 begin with n_x=r,n_y=N-r and move x to y, giving C sqrt(r(N-r+1)). Each magnitude lower-bounds ||[H,P]||2 independently of g. Identity/global-number residues are scalar within fixed N. For any diagonal F, ||[H,F]||F²=2 sum_unordered_edges |H_nm|² |F_m-F_n|², providing an independent weighted-graph norm check.

For general non-Hermitian A, the leakage factor is K_(A†)†K_B, where K_X=QXW, not K_A†K_B. The exact-isometry bound is ||D(A,B)||2 <= ||K_(A†)||2 ||K_B||2. For arbitrary W the direct identity D=W†AQBW still holds, while K_(A†)†K_B=W†AQ²BW; the difference is W†A(Q-Q²)BW. Set E=W†W-I. Then K_(A†)†K_B=D+A_c E B_c, so D=K_(A†)†K_B-A_c E B_c and ||D||2 <= ||K_(A†)||2 ||K_B||2 + ||A_c||2 ||E||2 ||B_c||2. Report this correction/isometry residual rather than claiming approximate W is exactly isometric. A=1,W=sqrt(1+epsilon) is a fixed one-dimensional counterexample to claiming exact positivity from approximate orthonormality: the subtraction defect is negative for positive epsilon.
