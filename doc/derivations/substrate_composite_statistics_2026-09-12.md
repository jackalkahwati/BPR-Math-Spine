## Reviewed tensor and neutrality conventions

The inherited single-site convention is c_i=(product_(j<i) Z_j)b_i, Z_j=1-2n_j. Independent review gives the ascending-product target C_S=(-1)^[r(r-1)/2] (product_(j in J(S)) Z_j)(product_(i in S) b_i), where J(S) contains sites outside S with an odd number of selected sites strictly to their right. Its original tensor support is S union J(S); the global phase cannot be dropped when comparing matrices. Even-degree composites can retain strings across interior gaps; odd degree also retains a prefix before the first selected site. These statements concern a fixed site ordering, not a proof of relativistic locality.

For inherited q=L the binary neutral subspace consists of vacuum and full filling. ## Mixed products and independent exact oracle

For disjoint supports of sizes r,s, both C_S C_T=(-1)^(rs) C_T C_S and C_S C_T†=(-1)^(rs) C_T† C_S hold. If annihilator supports overlap then C_S C_T=0, so that zero alone cannot diagnose grading. Mixed overlapping products require occupation projectors, not the disjoint sign rule alone.

Freeze the exact signed-bit oracle independently of the tensor formula: a single annihilator c_i on occupied bit i clears it and multiplies by (-1)^popcount(current_bits & ((1<<i)-1)); a creator on empty bit i sets it with the same current-prefix sign. Forbidden occupation kills the column. C_S C_T† acts first with creators in ASCENDING T, then annihilators in DESCENDING S. C_T† C_S acts descending S first, then ascending T. Use the changing intermediate bits, never the original parity for every operation. Empty factor products are identity internally, although public composite supports remain nonempty.

For hard-core Bose factors the same bit transitions have positive signs. Distinct-site ordinary Bose factors instead have square-root occupation coefficients on complete sectors. Exact binary matrices have only entries0,±1, so their algebra can be compared exactly; floating Bose square-root products require explicitly labeled numerical residuals.

## Derivation details and scoped conclusions

The tensor phase follows from bZ=-b and Zb=b: in ascending C_S each selected pair contributes one minus sign when parity factors are collected. Every unselected site contributes Z precisely when it lies below an odd number of selected sites. Tensor factors at S are nonzero b, factors at J are nontrivial Z, and all other factors are identity; X detects Z sites while Z detects b sites. Thus S union J is minimal support, not merely an upper bound. This proof uses the original tensor algebra and the fixed ordering, not a periodic-distance prescription.

For disjoint composites, interchanging r annihilators with s other annihilators or creators entails rs elementary CAR swaps and no contraction. With overlap, isolate each shared local factor. The identities b b†=1-n and b† b=n yield the vacancy and occupancy branches, while bZ=-b and Zb†=-b† give exactly alpha in the forward tensor formula. In the reverse formula the selected-site parity factors act on the opposite side and give no additional phase. Identical supports cancel the overall phases, giving the two stated self-products. Squaring requires b_x squared at every selected site and therefore vanishes. For r>1 a binary state with some but not all selected sites occupied is killed by both self-products; their sum cannot be identity. For r=1 empty and occupied projectors exhaust the local space, providing the positive CAR control.

Number charge follows by summing [n_x,c_y]=-delta_xy c_y over the r factors. A composite maps number N to N-r. If both source and target are divisible by q then r must be divisible by q. At q=L only N=0,L survive in the complete binary space, and full support is the sole nonempty pure-annihilator degree connecting them. No conclusion about dynamical binding follows from that selection rule.

For ordinary Bose factors, commute distinct sites and use a†a=n and aa†=n+1 locally. On vacuum each selected site is created once with amplitude1 and then again with amplitude sqrt2. Their product is sqrt(2^r), establishing nonnilpotency without exceeding total number2r. A complete-sector rectangular map is essential to this proof; projecting every intermediate occupation to0 or1 would remove precisely the witness being tested. These algebraic distinctions neither introduce an interaction nor establish microscopic physical fermions.
