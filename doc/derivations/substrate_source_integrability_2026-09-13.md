## Independently derived mathematics

All indices are periodic. The complete fixed-number occupation basis is ascending lexicographic, beginning `(0,...,0,N)`; dimensions for L=N=3,4,5 are10,35,126. Set `T_x=a†_(x+1)a_x`, `U_x=g n_x(n_x-1)/2`, `V_x=-C(exp(iA_x)T_x+exp(-iA_x)T_x†)` and `j_x=iC(exp(iA_x)T_x-exp(-iA_x)T_x†)`. The local energy is `h_y=U_y+a V_(y-1)+b V_y`, with a incoming and b outgoing. Thus improved means a=1/4,b=3/4, not reversed.

The first derivatives are `H_fy=h_y`, `H_Ax=-w_x j_x`, and `H_vy=n_y`. The only nonzero operator contacts are `H_fyAx=H_Axfy=-(b delta_xy+a delta_(y,x+1))j_x` and `H_AxAz=-delta_xz w_x V_x`. The ff,fv,vv,Av contacts vanish analytically; their completed ground-energy Hessian blocks need not vanish.

For a simple isolated ground eigenvalue, put `u_na=<n|H_a|0>` and `Delta_n=E_n-E_0>0`. Differentiating the eigenvalue equation gives `K_ab=C_ab-S_ab`, where `C_ab=<H_ab>` and `S_ab=2 Re sum_(n>0) conjugate(u_na) u_nb/Delta_n`. Retain every excited state even at excited degeneracies. The real matrix S is positive semidefinite and the connected contribution is -S; the contact-completed K need not be negative semidefinite. There is no Hessian of a selected arbitrary vector in an unresolved ground space. Nonzero flux invalidates the inherited zero-flux Perron–Frobenius uniqueness argument; numerical screens below are heuristic, not uniqueness proofs.

For `G_theta=sum theta_x n_x`, `U_theta=exp(iG_theta)` and `(Btheta)_x=theta_(x+1)-theta_x`, occupation phases prove `H[f,A+Btheta,v]=U_theta H[f,A,v] U_theta†`. Infinitesimally `H_(A.Btheta)=i[G_theta,H]`. Inserting `<n|H_(A.Btheta)|0>=-i Delta_n<n|G_theta|0>` into the spectral formula gives `C_(alpha,A.Btheta)=S_(alpha,A.Btheta)`. Consequently `K_AA B=K_fA B=K_vA B=0`. Neither contact nor spectral contribution is separately forced to zero. On the ring the zero-sum link subspace is range(B), so locally `K_AA=E_PhiPhi 11^T`, `K_fA=E_fPhi 1^T`, where `Phi=sum A_x`; uniform total flux is gauge invariant, not a gauge null; this does not supply physical calibration. These identities are checked, not imposed by projection.

The exact identity `sum n_x=NI` implies `E(f,A,v+kappa*1)=E(f,A,v)+kappa*N`, hence `K_(alpha,v)1=0` but first derivative N, not zero. A constant f direction is not generally a Hessian null. At uniform f and v=0 it has first derivative H and zero excited transitions but can have a nonzero mixed fA contact.

Direct commutation gives `i[H,n_y]=w_(y-1)j_(y-1)-w_y j_y`. A stationary state therefore has constant weighted current `w_x<j_x>`, not necessarily constant bare current. For evolution under this same differentiable time-dependent source family, `d<H>/dt=sum dot(f_y)<h_y>-sum dot(A_x)w_x<j_x>+sum dot(v_y)<n_y>`. No extra dot(w) term is added: it is already in the dot(f) term. A time-dependent gauge transformation requires `A'=A+Btheta, v'=v-dot(theta)`. These are external-source identities, not covariant stress conservation or metric dynamics.

The two partitions obey `H_improved-H_symmetric=(1/4)sum(f_x-f_(x+1))V_x`. Uniform backgrounds share H and its spectrum, but not all f derivatives; modulated backgrounds need not share H. The36 labeled controls contain27 distinct background Hamiltonians. The implementation will nevertheless construct one independently owned background per labeled case, sequentially, never retain36 dense systems or share derivative caches between cases.

Use dimensionless coordinates `q=(f,A,u)` with `u=v/C` and energy `Ecal=E/C`. Every numerical derivative, contact, Hessian and finite difference in the new API refers to `Hcal=H/C`. Thus `Kq=D^T Kphysical D/C`, `D=diag(I,I,C I)`. All f,A,u coordinates are dimensionless. The public controls use C=1, but private normalization fixtures use other allowed C to expose errors hidden by that value.
