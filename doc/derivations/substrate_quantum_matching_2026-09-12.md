The equally energetic -3pi/L partner is omitted. The choice of one partner is recorded, never treated as an isolated symmetry-invariant window.

For an ascending site subset X of size N and ascending column subset I of size N, set W[X,I]=det(U[X,I]). Use ascending binary-bit ordering for the occupation rows and fixed column-combination order. By the Cauchy–Binet identity, sum_X conjugate(det(U[X,I])) det(U[X,J]) equals det((U†U)[I,J])=delta_IJ.
