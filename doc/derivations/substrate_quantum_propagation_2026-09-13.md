## Independently derived and cross-reviewed mathematics

Work on the finite-particle core of the full site Fock tensor product before restricting to fixed N. An onsite term cannot enlarge support; a bond commutator can add at most one neighboring site and vanishes for disjoint support. Thus A_r=ad_H^r(dx) is supported in the radius-r graph ball. For d=dist(x,y), [A_r,dy]=0 at r<d. All factors preserve total number, so restriction preserves their products and commutators.

The one-particle hopping spectrum is −2C cos(2πk/L); its N-boson lift has norm2CN. The interaction satisfies0≤D≤N(N−1)I/2. With C>0,g≥0, ||H||≤BN and ||dx||=1, giving ||A_r||≤(2BN)^r and ||[A_r,dy]||≤2(2BN)^r. The norm-convergent Heisenberg series has coefficients (it)^r/r!, yielding the approved envelope. For adjacent x,y, [[H,dx],dy]=C(a†y ax+a†x ay)/N²; its first time coefficient is +it.

For the degree-m commutator polynomial K^[m](t), the integral Taylor remainder of unitary conjugation gives ||K(t)−K^[m](t)||≤2(2BN|t|)^(m+1)/(m+1)!, without an exponential factor. This bound is on exact operators, not floating assembly errors. Degree6 at fixed t=.001,.01 was cross-reviewed and frozen for independent tests.

For the free control g=0, U_ab(t)=L^−1 Σk exp(2πik(a−b)/L) exp(2iCt cos(2πk/L)). With dΓ(M)=Σab M_ab a†a ab, dx(t)=dΓ(U†PxU)/N and Kxy(t)=dΓ([U†PxU,Py])/N². The one-particle commutator has eigenvalues ±i s sqrt(1−s²), s=|Uxy|, and bosonic occupation of an extremal eigenmode gives the scalar norm Fxy(t)=s sqrt(1−s²)/N. The Fourier lift supplies an independent complete-sector oracle without many-body diagonalization. A small permanent-lift fixture can additionally test normalization. These identities were confirmed by the separate adversarial check.

The two evolved densities are positive contractions, so a sharper general cap1/2 follows by centering both by I/2. The approved conservative cap2 is intentionally unchanged; reaching it describes envelope saturation, not physical commutator saturation. `_capped_tail(z,distance)` accepts finite built-in/NumPy real z≥0 and integer distance1..4, with strict type validation. It returns `{status,value,partial_sum,remainder_bound,terms,reason}`. Status is `structural_zero`, `below_stipulated_cap`, `trivial_cap`, or `numerical_unavailable`. Zero z returns exact value0, partial_sum0, remainder_bound0, terms0. z≥distance returns value2 immediately with partial_sum/remainder_bound null, terms0 and its analytic saturation reason. This branch precedes powers/exp, so large finite z is safe.

For0<z<distance, start a=z^distance/distance! by successive real multiplication/division. Maintain a positive sum s through order m, initially m=distance and terms1. If s≥1, return cap2. Otherwise calculate next=a*z/(m+1), R=next/(1−z/(m+2)); once R≤8*binary64_eps*s, return min(2,2*(s+R)), with s and R recorded and status determined by whether this value reaches2. Otherwise add next, set a=next,m=m+1,terms+=1 and repeat, at most256 accumulated terms. Evaluate cap/stopping checks after the256th term, never add a257th. On s≥1 return partial_sum=s,remainder_bound=null,terms=current count and reason `partial sum reaches stipulated cap`. Before structural-zero/saturation branching, detect positive source z converted to binary64 zero or finite source z converted to infinity; these representability failures return numerical_unavailable. Detect lost positive starting-term, next-term and remainder intermediates. Positive-term underflow, nonfinite arithmetic or exhaustion yields numerical_unavailable, not structural zero or a truncated tail. On unavailability value is null; partial_sum/remainder_bound retain only finite successfully computed values, otherwise null; terms counts actually accumulated terms and reason is mandatory. Invalid type/domain instead raises TypeError/ValueError; only valid-input numerical failure returns an unavailable record. No exp-minus-polynomial subtraction, clipping or normal/subnormal domain exclusion. Exact arithmetic gives T≤s+R; floating evaluation is explicitly heuristic, not outward-rounded certification. The returned value is a conservative evaluation of the approved envelope, not a claim to exactly equal its infinite series.
