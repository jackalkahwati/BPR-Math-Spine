## Operators and response

Set T_x=a†_(x+1)*a_x, V_x=-C*(T_x+T_x†), V=sum V_x, and outgoing j_x=i*C*(T_x-T_x†). With dot O=i[H,O], dot n_x=j_(x-1)-j_x. Fourier convention rho_k=L^(-1/2)*sum exp(-ikx)*(n_x-1), j_k=L^(-1/2)*sum exp(-ikx)*j_x gives dot rho_k=(exp(-ik)-1)*j_k. Uniform rho0 is structurally zero at fixed N=L, but uniform current/flux response need not vanish.

Define chi_AB(z)=-i*integral_0^infinity exp(izt)<[A(t),B]>dt, Im z>0. In a stationary pure source ground state, chi_AB=sum_n A_0n*B_n0/(z-Delta_n)-B_0n*A_n0/(z+Delta_n). The two mixed-observable numerators are generally different. When B=A† they become |A_0n|² and |A_n0|² respectively, not necessarily the same weight for non-Hermitian A; otherwise neither substitution by an absolute square is justified. Connected subtraction removes elastic ground components without changing commutators. Retain actual eigenvalue denominators, not grouped representatives.

Integration by parts gives z*chi_AB=<[A,B]>+i*chi_(dot A,B) and stationarity gives z*chi_AB=<[A,B]>-i*chi_(A,dot B). Combining continuity with these equations gives density/current Ward identities including the equal-time contact. Check both sides independently using operator commutators and full retarded response. For a real reflection-symmetric source ground state, M1(k)=<rho_k†*(H-E0)*rho_k>=-(1-cos k)*<V>/L. Derive this from half the expected double commutator; the unsymmetrized formula requires the equality of k and -k measures, not arbitrary states.

Writing q_k=exp(-ik)-1, the contacts are <[rho_k,j_-k]>=-i*q_k*<V>/L and <[j_k,rho_-k]>=i*q_k.conjugate()*<V>/L. The combined identity is z²*chi_(rho_k,rho_-k)=2*(1-cos k)*[chi_(j_k,j_-k)-<V>/L]. It must be checked using independently evaluated responses, not by defining one response from the identity. At k0 do not divide by q_k. The mixed two-level fixture H=diag(0,Delta), A=sigma_x,B=sigma_y gives i/(z-Delta)+i/(z+Delta), a control that rules out replacing both mixed numerators by squared magnitudes.

## External flux and gauge covariance

For total flux Phi put H(Phi)=-C*sum[exp(i*Phi/L)*T_x+h.c.]+gD. H(0) must equal the existing H, H'(0)=-J/L with J=sum j_x, and H''(0)=-V/L². For a resolved nondegenerate ground state,

E0''(0)=[-<V>-2*sum_(n>0)|<n|J|0>|²/(En-E0)]/L².

At zero flux, the complete fixed-N occupation graph is connected and H has nonpositive off-diagonal hopping for C>0. Perron-Frobenius applied to a sufficiently shifted sI-H proves the finite-dimensional ground vector is unique and strictly positive for finite g>=0; its invariance under occupation-basis permutations induced by actual ring symmetries (translation/reflection) follows, not invariance under arbitrary site permutations. This exact uniqueness does not ensure its gap is numerically resolved at arbitrary scales, or prove uniqueness at all flux.

Let K=-<V>. The discrete diamagnetic inequality follows by replacing a trial vector by its componentwise modulus, removing hopping phases and not increasing energy, so E0(Phi)>=E0(0). The real zero-flux ground vector gives trial energy E0(0)+K*(1-cos(Phi/L)). Hence 0<=E0(Phi)-E0(0)<=K*(1-cos(Phi/L)). For the centered finite difference D_h, 0<=D_h<=2*K*(1-cos(h/L))/h²<=K/L², and 0<=E0''(0)<=K/L². These are exact model bounds, not floating-point certificates or bounds on |D_h-E0''|. Local analytic perturbation gives D_h=E0''+h²*E0''''/12+O(h⁴); a certified error would require an independently bounded fourth derivative over the interval. Retain finite-difference convergence as diagnostic only.

Report diamagnetic and paramagnetic pieces separately, their difference, conditioning and flux normalization. This finite-ring curvature is not automatically a thermodynamic stiffness, physical modulus or superfluid classification. A difference of nearly equal pieces can be numerically unavailable. Finite-difference refinement is an independent diagnostic, not a proof of its truncation error.

For a fixed site phase theta_x, U_theta=exp(i*sum theta_x*n_x) sends T_x to exp(i*(theta_(x+1)-theta_x))*T_x. Thus link phases A'_x=A_x+theta_(x+1)-theta_x give H(A')=U_theta*H(A)*U_theta†. Their summed flux agrees; densities commute with U_theta while currents transform with their phase-dependent definitions. This external source covariance is not invariance of an unchanged H under arbitrary local phases and does not establish a physical Gauss constraint. A time-dependent transformation of states would also require i*dot U_theta*U_theta†=-sum_x dot theta_x*n_x in the transformed Hamiltonian; the present static-source construction supplies no such dynamical gauge sector. For moving uniform total flux to the final link, theta_x=-x*Phi/L is an explicit independent redistribution fixture.
